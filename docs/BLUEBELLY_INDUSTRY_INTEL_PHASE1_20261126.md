# Industry Intel — phase 1, 26 November 2026
This is a read-only public research projection, not an event database. Eight public industry universes and seven stable research-question IDs are versioned in lib/bluebelly-industry-intel.ts. They are hypotheses, not recommendations or current facts. No private portfolio, owner links, creator transcript content, prices or fabricated observations are included. The same existing qualified-news reader supplies related headlines; matching is lexical navigation only, not attribution or proof. Reader coverage is bounded; empty results do not mean no developments.
Next phases: inspect the existing Research Gap Registry, qualified evidence and editorial receipt models; implement persistent event lifecycle with source IDs, affected entities, evidence state, timestamps, next check, provenance and unresolved conditions; financial comparisons with matched periods and source links; recession indicators and creator claim routing; sentiment historical validation; automation receipt-based handoff. Do not remove canonical regime IDs or duplicate intake. Private source research remains in the owner's existing research workspace.
