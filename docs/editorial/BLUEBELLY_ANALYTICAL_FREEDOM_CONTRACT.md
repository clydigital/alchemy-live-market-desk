# Bluebelly — Analytical Freedom Contract (4 Nov 2026)

## Editorial objective
Bluebelly should be rigorous about what happened and adventurous about what may happen next. Missing complete historical evidence is not a reason to suppress a useful, explicitly conditional market interpretation.

## Three claim lanes
1. **Verified observation:** what happened, when, what was reported, and the directly supported source. Require exact source, time, metric basis and attribution for numerical surprises, prices, quotes and realised reactions.
2. **Analyst interpretation:** a reasoned mechanism connecting established observations to market incentives or positioning. Label as interpretation, identify assumptions, strongest alternative explanation, and distinguish causality from correlation.
3. **Forward scenario:** what markets may be looking for, what could happen if X occurs, which assets may benefit/suffer, what would falsify the scenario, and the next observable test. Clearly mark as conditional, not as a market-consensus fact.

## Publishing rule
A sourced factual anchor plus transparent conditional analysis can make a useful article even when the full consensus series, prior comparable or measured reaction is missing. Do not label an outcome BEAT/MISS, quote an expectation, assert a causal market reaction, or claim a probability without matching evidence. State the missing comparison once, naturally; do not fill public articles with internal research-gap diagnostics. Missing evidence stays in the private investigation queue.

## Editorial sequence
- **What happened?** A concise source-dated anchor, or state clearly that the event is upcoming.
- **Why now?** The tension, incentive, positioning or contradiction that makes it interesting.
- **What is the market trying to decide?** Competing hypotheses, without pretending to know consensus.
- **Scenario fork:** bull/base/bear or 2–3 meaningful conditional paths; show mechanism, beneficiaries/losers and disconfirming signals. Avoid fake precision.
- **The next test:** a dated release, price/credit/breadth signal or company disclosure, and what changes the view.

## Application
- **MacroPulse:** surface emerging hypotheses as attributed research leads; separate observed price action from possible drivers.
- **Research Investigation:** actively test scenario premises, countercases and missing primary evidence; do not gate all forward interpretation on exhaustive evidence.
- **Writing Brain:** prefer decision-useful narrative and explicit conditional forks over repeated 'unverified' boilerplate. Preserve LY voice, British English and author discretion.
- **AI Bubble / Creator:** treat projections and creator views as hypotheses; test against earnings, cash conversion, capex, debt and market data, never upgrade attributed commentary to verified fact.
- **Public Bluebelly:** show source-grounded anchors and clearly signposted interpretation/scenarios; preserve canonical admission, owner review, public/private boundary and exact historical editions.

## Regression acceptance
1. Incomplete consensus + verified issuer capex: a conditional cash-flow/valuation analysis is allowed, but no invented beat/miss.
2. Observed price move + unproven catalyst: alternative drivers and conditional mechanism allowed, definitive attribution prohibited.
3. Upcoming central bank decision: conditional hawkish/dovish branches allowed, invented pricing/probabilities prohibited.
4. Creator prediction: attributed scenario allowed, not promoted to a verified outcome.
5. Public copy: no raw internal research-gap checklist; private gap queue still records missing evidence.
6. No additional scheduled task, research engine, canonical writer, automatic publication privilege or unreviewed change to live production.
