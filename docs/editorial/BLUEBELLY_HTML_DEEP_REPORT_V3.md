## Mandatory writing process

**Pass 1 · Source packet before writing.** Retrieve original issuer/EDGAR/central-bank releases, the most recent complete and partial MacroPulse+Gap Posts and eligible creator transcripts. Tag each event as `ORIGINAL_VERIFIED` / `CORROBORATED` / `RESEARCH_LEAD` / `UNKNOWN`. Retain URLs, publisher, period, publication/event/observation timestamps, underlying instrument and source currency/unit. A source claimed as original must have actually been accessed, not just named.

**Pass 2 · Financial judgement.** For each important market or company candidate prepare:
- pitch: what became unexpectedly new?
- economics: revenue, operating profit, operating cash flow, cash capex, FCF, financed assets, dilution or yields **only where period and basis verified**;
- catch: best quantified alternative or contract risk;
- negotiation: which upcoming disclosure or observation would revise the verdict?
- independent price tape, if available, or explicitly UNKNOWN; an SEC S-1 is still material without tradable public equity.

**Pass 3 · Storytelling.** Lead with a contradiction and one or two actually sourced numbers, follow the true chronological disclosure sequence or labelled decision order. Embed 2–3 useful jargon lessons where they unlock the scene. A main visual must teach economics, not just decorate. Layer with substantive, optional `<details>` source/economic due diligence and clear conditional **base → test → challenge** ending. Don't imitate Shark Tank hosts, invent dialogue, or impersonate the author's private bias.

**Pass 4 · Claim-level quality gates.** Reject future-dated items, recycled press syndication, missing metric period, invented issuer guidance, mismatched cash-flow periods, unsupported historical chart claims, and implied event-driven stock causality. Reject fake X post IDs; click-to-load actual original URLs only with verified relevance, date, fallback. For each top material original-source candidate, the three-angle decision must either select it or name why it lost.

**Pass 5 · Portable release and proof.** Self-contained HTML/JS/CSS, 390px and 1280px Chromium smoke checks where the tool exists, no horizontal overflow or console exceptions; browser embed consent; no keys/cookies. Upload the HTML raw to private Drive, retain original source/vintage and immutable edition date, read back file MIME and parent. If unable, report `DRIVE HTML UNAVAILABLE` without pretending upload happened.

## Report-specific editorial minimums

**Market briefing:** one dominant tension and ~3–5 original-dated scenes *where available*. Explain pricing/financial mechanics, opposing interpretation, explicit unknowns, watchpoints and a ranked source ledger. Don't equate headline transaction size with cash received.

**Writing Brain:** exactly three real canonical ranked ideas, each supported by a detailed proposed narrative, decision-ready statistics, risk objection, conditional indicator chart specification, exact next evidence and publication `DRAFT-READY / RESEARCH-ONLY / HOLD` status. Show strongest excluded qualified filing as a **separate noncanonical candidate**; never quietly rewrite the immutable 09:00 slate.

**Creator batch:** one coherent per-creator episode from multiple transcript-backed videos, publication order, changing thesis, provider and retrieval provenance, original quote or attributable claim, independent issuer comparison, and 2–3 paths with falsifiers. StockedUp 26 Oct claims proposed 'ENT' while SEC/filing reporting indicates 'ANT'; show disagreement and final prospectus caveat rather than silently rewriting.

**News audit:** distinguish *collection volume* from *qualified original-source recall*. Preserve MacroPulse ID, Research Gap eligible/read/unread counts, Writing Brain chosen/excluded candidates and source ages. Show specific failed stage, not merely 'the AI missed it'.
