# Bluebelly — Uncertainty, Positivity & Supply/Demand Decision Framework
**Approved editorial operating standard · 12 November 2026 · evidence-first, conditional interpretation**

## Purpose
Every material Newsroom, Key Story, Power Stack and Regime Watch research item must answer: **what did the decision signal, did it increase or reduce uncertainty, how do supply and demand change, and what should happen to the market versus what the market actually did?**

Do not equate "uncertainty is bad" with an automatic bearish trade. Risk premia often widen under unexpected unresolved uncertainty; some asset classes and hedges benefit, an apparently bad *known* outcome may remove uncertainty, and a stronger growth result may support earnings while increasing inflation/rate pressure.

## Minimum market-story reasoning sequence
1. **Event/decision**: exact original issuer/statistical/regulator source, source release clock, measures, period, observed or proposed or projected. The published fact is not the explanation.
2. **What the decision signals**: is the decision evidence of confidence, stress, precaution, policy commitment, a supply bottleneck or a change in demand? Identify whose decision, whose incentives and whether it is binding.
3. **Uncertainty direction**: rising / falling / mixed / unassessed; identify which *specific unknown* was resolved and which new uncertainties are opened. Explain implications for risk premia and valuation **conditionally**, not as observed causality.
4. **Market positivity is asset-specific**: beneficiaries, negatively exposed parties and neutral exposures. Distinguish the economy, physical product, equity, debt, currency and commodity. A shared event may raise some expected cash flows while compressing a multiple.
5. **Physical and financial transmission**: separate physical supply, physical demand, product inventory, logistical constraints, price elasticity, financing supply, investor/ETF demand for shares, interest/refinancing cost and cash conversion. Do not confuse passive equity selling with changes to BTC or uranium production.
6. **Pre-event consensus/textbook expectation**: collect independently published survey, futures or expectations with provenance and as-of. If unavailable, mark textbook conditional only; never invent a surprise.
7. **Observed market response and divergence**: matched prices, units, as-of, separate 5m/30m/4h/close/next session. Expected up/down vs actual up/down; classify divergence yes/no/partial **only with matched observations**. Reuters narrative ≠ isolated causality.
8. **Competing explanations**: at least one plausible alternative (already priced, term premium, short covering, energy shock, positioning, sector rotation) and evidence for/against.
9. **Next falsifier**: quantitative or documentary observation, with issuer/official source, time and clear change-of-view threshold where available.
10. **Publication state**: original-source news can appear as SOURCE_ONLY with "NOT ASSESSED" but not as a reviewed market verdict. The existing Newsroom/Regime/Dossier approval boundaries remain in force. Never autonomously promote creator, X or private Notion analysis into canonical status.

## Case distinctions
- **EIA 10 Nov gas**: higher forecast US inventories lowers near-term domestic gas-scarcity concern; diesel/refined fuels can remain tight. Not a universal bullish energy signal. Both price reactions unmeasured.
- **NVIDIA NVLink Fusion**: potential networking ecosystem advantage while specialised NPUs partly substitute for GPUs. Orders, utilisation and true efficiency not yet measured.
- **Jensen Huang**: a verified board-ordered investigation and attributed subsequent deliberation increase governance uncertainty, not verified changes to GPU orders. Watch original board statement/8-K, outcomes and 17 Nov earnings separately. A personal allegation is not an established corporate finding.
- **CoreWeave**: higher compute revenue with high financing cost means physical AI demand and equity-return economics can diverge. Expected customer deposits are not received cash.
- **Alphabet bonds**: strong orders indicate potential bond-investor appetite, not proof of closed financing or profitable AI capex.
- **Siemens Energy**: large backlog and pre-tax cash flow support physical equipment demand but deliveries, cancellation rights and advances matter.
- **MSCI November index review**: passive share supply/demand changes are not production or consumption changes for underlying assets.

## Operational acceptance tests
- A source-only notice cannot produce economic polarity or market cause by headline matching.
- A review packet has a valid original source and all explicit uncertainty, beneficiaries/pressured, physical and financial transmission, expected/observed reaction, alternative and falsifier fields.
- Missing matched tape sets divergence=UNTESTED.
- No stale investigation is described as a current board outcome.
- Newsroom and Key Stories show distinctions; historical thesis reader does not manufacture event evidence.
- Research backlog stays owner-only; dated manual research stays distinct from verified public NewsBrief, canonical Regime or private portfolio actions.
