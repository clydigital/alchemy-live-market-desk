# Bluebelly daily delivery contract — 3 November 2026

**Purpose:** restore the four base jobs: (1) complete Daily Intelligence; (2) ingest fresh original releases; (3) keep the shared Newsroom current; (4) apply the bounded market consequence framework. **No new task, research writer, public AI call or automatic unreviewed publication.**

## Acceptance, not merely activity

- **One complete private Daily Intelligence HTML per completed US equity weekday session**, stored under My Desk with matching ET report day and permanent saved-edition readback. A successful MacroPulse/Research Gap run or an OB/COTD idea is *not* a complete daily report. If the US market has not yet closed, label the most recent completed session and any premarket addendum explicitly. A private draft is not a public article.
- **Public Daily Market Report** at `/daily-report`: a *rolling, source-qualified reader* showing up to five distinct developments, two to three dated upcoming catalysts and a plain-English conditional consequence for each topic. Keep original facts, timestamps, future vs released status and links to the complete Key Story. Historical `?edition=` selectors fail closed; never substitute the current report for an immutable historic edition. A rolling report is not a claim of a saved daily editorial edition.
- **Fresh releases**: existing source-qualified original Motion / official-press receipts feed the Newsroom and Today. Source-reviewed macro and issuer outcomes that have already passed existing original-source gates also appear without requiring new canonical Motion acceptance. Date-only earnings do not receive invented release clocks or analyst-consensus beats.
- **Newsroom**: make availability visible. If zero eligible fresh official records exist in the preceding 48 hours, say so and keep earlier reviewed items visibly dated; never pad the feed with unconfirmed creator opinions or reframe previews as verified outcomes.
- **Market consequence**: each eligible brief states what happened, possible economic/business impact, the specific unresolved outcome, what was expected (or not verified) and the actual named-asset reaction (or not measured). Keep longer numerical discussion, pre-release comparator, source vintage, next test and countercase below. No automatic `priced in` or inferred cause of share moves.

## Owner and system boundaries

Existing MacroPulse (hourly), Research Investigation (08:40/20:40 MYT), Writing Brain (09:30 MYT Mon–Sat), Creator and AI 16-company schedules remain unchanged. The scheduled private HTML fallback and original research crons remain inside current server architecture. These actions should **not** automatically approve public articles, alter canonical Dossier/System 2, create a sixth task, disclose owner-only HTML to public hosts, or rewrite prior revisions.

## Operating checks for each day

1. Research runs: record latest scheduled run status, published/intake source count and original/source timestamp separately. A green job with zero newly accepted official release receipts is **research active, fresh-publication coverage absent** until source checks establish otherwise.
2. After the completed US session: verify the most recent My Desk private HTML edition has the expected session day, valid evidence ledger, at most five main decision topics, source-vintage/consensus limitations and a stable archive readback. Owner-only fallback must not mask a missing full editorial report.
3. Public website: confirm `/daily-report` has up to five qualified topic stories, `/newsroom` shows verified original/report provenance and the release rail, `/` reuses the same editorial admission, and `/key-stories` preserves full details.
4. Verify response freshness across ET/MYT boundaries and an empty-source condition; carry forward prior reviewed articles as **earlier research**, not brand-new news.
5. For exceptions, open a concrete source-ingestion or delivery gap with timestamps and failed data contract. No automatic weaker verification.
