# Bluebelly Market Consequence Standard v1.0

**Status:** Editorial and deterministic quality contract, not a new evidence authority or second reasoning engine. **Owner:** Existing MacroPulse → Research Gap → canonical Dossier/System 2 → approved editorial publication. **Date:** 3 November 2026. **Surface:** Bluebelly public Newsroom, Key Stories, Daily Report and owner-only writing/presenter drafts.

## The reader's experience: brief first, receipts underneath

Every significant source-qualified story SHOULD open under the headline with a **Quick Market Brief** of one to three straightforward sentences:

- Say what happened and the likely practical effect on a named business, sector, economy or asset, conditional where necessary.
- Name the exact uncertainty when it is relevant: what remains undecided, not a generic fear label.
- Separate economic direction from asset-price direction. A good earnings report can coincide with falling shares.
- Use no unsourced price move, causal certainty or "priced in" claim. Do not pad a minor fact to manufacture a consequence.

Below the brief, preserve the **Full Story**: original releases, previous/baseline, comparably measured pre-event expectations, actual and guidance, cash/economic route, matched market reactions if available, competing explanations, valuation where justified, remaining gaps and next tests. Never trade detail for readability: **the brief is a door into the evidence, not a replacement for it**. Render details directly below or in an accessible disclosure; keep mobile readability and provenance.

## Five sequential checks: System 1's bounded job

1. **Event:** What literally happened, when, to whom? Require original source and source/event clock.
2. **Economic effect:** Whose earnings, costs, demand, revenue, credit conditions, cash flow or policy constraints could change? Favourable / adverse / mixed / unknown **for a named entity**. No general market verdict from a company-specific fact.
3. **Uncertainty:** Which consequential outcome is harder or easier to estimate? Increased / decreased / unchanged / unknown, with the unresolved question and next possible resolution. Avoid assigning "fear" from the headline alone.
4. **Expectations:** What was known or priced *before* this event? Freeze consensus/provider, time, metric, period and accounting basis. Missing data must say **expectations not verified**; do not assert "priced in".
5. **Price response:** What did the named instrument actually do in a measured, dated window? A session change is not automatically an event-window reaction. If observation absent say **market reaction unmeasured**.

**Economic effect ≠ price reaction.** Each can be favourable, adverse, mixed or unknown independently. Price is an observation, not evidence that the preferred explanation caused it.

## Escalation into existing System 2 (not an automatic thesis update)

Keep System 1 short for routine qualified events. Escalate an investigation when one or more conditions is met:

- A material verified positive economic result accompanies contrary asset-price behaviour, or vice versa.
- Specific credible uncertainty could affect broad earnings, inflation, credit or liquidity.
- A significant actual-versus-consensus/guidance surprise is verified.
- Meaningful cross-asset contradictions or mutually incompatible original reports remain.
- A persistent Story/Regime thesis may require reassessment.

For a contradiction, System 2 may test **at most three initially relevant explanations**, chosen from: stronger pre-event expectations / peak earnings, changed forward guidance, valuation multiple and earnings estimates, real/nominal yields, positioning/options, sector-wide or market-wide move, another simultaneous catalyst. Each has support/against and a falsifiable next observation. Use **supported**, **plausible but unverified**, **contradicted**, **unknown**. **Cause not established** is a valid and often preferable verdict. Only the existing canonical Dossier/System 2 process can accept or revise a Story/Regime thesis.

## Specialised detail, not a universal template

- **Earnings:** reported/previous/pre-release expected on matched definitions; revenue, relevant segments, earnings, guidance, operating cash flow, *actual* cash capex, free cash flow and valuation (forward versus trailing, peer/historical basis) **only where verified**. Never infer cheapness from a drawdown or cash flow from operating profit.
- **Central banks:** previous/current decision and votes; separately attributable pre-event OIS/futures expectation; front and long yields and currency reactions measured over suitable windows. Hawkish/dovish always relative to the priced path.
- **Energy/geopolitics:** distinguish proposed from implemented policy, feared from observed supply/shipping disruption, crude from refined-product stress and insurance/freight cost; both escalation and resolution paths.
- **Elections:** historical post-election sample, date convention and methodology; proposed/actual policy and winners/losers; scenario branches and confidence without fabricated probabilities. No universal "post-midterm rally" rule.
- **Routine original news:** short verified event report may stand alone with explicit **research depth incomplete** rather than pretending a full analytical Story exists.

## Acceptance cases — source-dated, NOT simulated live quotes

**Company earnings with falling shares:** Economic performance may be favourable and share-price reaction adverse. Without comparable pre-event consensus, valuation or measured reaction, do NOT assert "peak earnings priced in"; open the relevant investigation only.

**Iran / oil / Hormuz:** Negotiations or threatened closures are not confirmed physical reopening or lost volume. Explain supply, freight and inflation *conditionally*, then check tanker passages, insurance and crude/products. A lower war-risk premium can relieve equity valuation pressure while persistent fuel inflation hurts margins.

**US midterms 2026:** Historical election patterns explain a potential uncertainty-release hypothesis, not a guaranteed return. Without confirmed 2026 election outcome, do not assign winners, policy law, reliable election probability or automatic SPX/DXY/yield reaction.

## Minimum editorial / engineering release gates

- [ ] Quick brief is understandable on its own and located above numerical detail.
- [ ] Each major economic effect names the affected entity and evidence or conditional basis.
- [ ] Uncertainty names a specific unresolved outcome, not simply "uncertain".
- [ ] Comparable previous / expected / actual numbers retain metric, unit, period, GAAP basis, source and pre-release vintage.
- [ ] Reported asset-price behaviour has instrument, observation time/window and provider.
- [ ] When business and price disagree, hypotheses are not promoted to proven causes.
- [ ] Detail stays available; missing depth is explicit rather than filler.
- [ ] Historical editions and source UUIDs remain frozen; owner information stays private.
- [ ] Full regression, TypeScript/build, authenticated/anonymous reader checks and production readback gate public rollout.

## Implementation discipline

This standard belongs alongside `docs/editorial/BLUEBELLY_STORY_DRIVEN_DAILY_REPORTS.md`, the Fundamentals Investor Test, the central-bank standard and the existing `bluebelly-content/1` read contract. It does **not** change canonical writers, schedules, source admissions, permissions or publication status by itself. Prefer pure read-model changes and a single owner branch. A PR, CI green, Notion page or preview are **not** proof of production publication.
