# Macro research taxonomy — coverage floors, not ceilings
Status: PROVISIONAL until validated against genuine transcripts from StockedUp, Wall Street Truth Bombs/Mark Malek, FXEvolution and TraderNick. Exclude Lucid Trading.

## Common fields for EVERY partition
What happened; time/source; what was expected; actual market reaction; causal mechanism; second-order transmission; competing explanation; relevant existing Regime/Story; Motion lead and next test; source/evidence status; missing discriminator; cross-compartment links; **Others / Emerging Connections** (unbounded discovery). No forced findings when nothing material changed.

## 1. US Treasuries and monetary policy
- Curve: 2Y, 5Y, 10Y, 20Y, 30Y; curve spreads, real yields, breakevens, duration and term premium (never infer term premium by subtracting TIPS).
- Treasury supply: auctions, tails, bid-to-cover, dealers/indirects, refunding, issuance and fiscal funding.
- Fed: decisions, speeches, dot plot, futures pricing, balance sheet, QT/QE, repo, reserves.
- Transmission: mortgages, corporate refinancing, dollar, gold, growth equities, housing.
- Others: foreign buyers, basis trades, pension demand, market plumbing, dislocations.

## 2. Economic health and catalysts
- Inflation: CPI, PCE, PPI, wages, services, goods, revisions, expectations.
- Labour: NFP, JOLTS, ADP, claims, unemployment, participation, hours, ECI.
- Activity: ISM/PMI, GDP, retail sales, confidence, consumption, inventories.
- Housing/households: affordability, delinquencies, savings, debt service, lending.
- Corporate: earnings, guidance, margins, capex, demand.
- Others: regional surveys, fiscal transfers, data quality, release calendar.

## 3. Energy and geopolitics
- Physical oil: Iran/Hormuz, Houthis/Red Sea, OPEC+, production, refineries, crude/product inventories.
- Products: diesel, gasoline, jet fuel, crack spreads only with matched contracts.
- Gas: Qatar/Hormuz LNG, TTF/JKM, shipping, storage, power.
- Policy/conflict: sanctions, IEA releases, infrastructure, insurance, freight.
- Transmission: inflation, industrial margins, food/fertiliser, growth and yields.
- Others: electricity grids, shipping rerouting, strategic stockpiles, geopolitical surprises.

## 4. AI, technology and financing
- Demand/supply: Nvidia, GPUs, HBM, DRAM, China/CXMT, export controls, hyperscalers.
- Economics: training versus inference costs, pricing competition, adoption, monetisation.
- Physical build: data centres, power, cooling, water, grid, equipment.
- Financing: capex commitments, debt, convertibles, vendor financing, private credit, credit spreads.
- Equity: concentration, valuation, earnings, breadth and semiconductor confirmation.
- Others: software displacement, productivity, China-US AI competition, regulation.

## 5. Credit, liquidity and market internals
- Credit: IG/HY/CCC spreads, defaults, maturities, private credit, bank lending.
- Funding: repo, collateral, dollar liquidity, cross-currency basis, leverage.
- Internals: SPX/Nasdaq/Russell/SMH breadth, equal weight, sectors, advance/decline.
- Volatility/positioning: VIX, MOVE, options, dealer positioning when sourced, ETF flows, buybacks, short interest.
- Others: forced deleveraging, funding squeezes, unusual divergences.

## 6. Global markets and flows
- China/HK: stimulus, credit, property, consumption, exports, AI, oil/diesel demand.
- Japan: BOJ, JGB curve, JPY, carry, repatriation.
- Europe/UK: ECB/BOE, sovereign yields, energy, industry, EUR/GBP.
- FX/reserves: DXY, major FX, gold, central bank buying, foreign Treasury demand.
- Others: EM capital flows, trade tariffs, sovereign stress, cross-border investment.

## 7. Final cross-market synthesis
Not another headline search. Identify central thesis, new/persisting/reversing developments, causal chain, observed confirmation, contradictory market behaviour, affected Regime/Story/Motion, top 1–3 actionable research questions, and what to exclude. Include **Others / Novel Relationships**.

## Creator taxonomy validation
For each genuine transcript record creator/video ID/timestamp, covered subcompartments, repeated mechanisms, neglected topics, and proposed additions. Track observed frequency and depth without turning creator claims into evidence. Do not claim taxonomy is validated until transcript samples are inspected.

## Coverage without compulsory output (review amendment)
- Each subcompartment is a **discovery prompt**, not a required daily finding or mandatory live data fetch.
- Mark NOT CHECKED, NO MATERIAL CHANGE, SOURCE UNAVAILABLE or FINDING; distinguish unavailable data from stable conditions.
- Check high-impact cross-field developments even if they do not fit a predefined category. Others / Emerging Connections is always open.
- Collapse repeated events into one primary finding, then link their rates, credit, energy, equity and FX implications across compartments.
- Creator transcript sampling refines future coverage but is asynchronous; missing videos must never prevent current market research or publication.
