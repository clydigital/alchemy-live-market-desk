# Original Live Desk: Recommended Editorial-Brain Blueprint

## Decision

The Original should not be rebuilt as one monolithic dashboard. It should combine the strongest modules from all three variants:

- **Homepage and daily decisions:** Story Command Desk.
- **Economic-release analysis:** Release Ledger.
- **Cross-release synthesis:** Macro Operating System.
- **Persistent memory:** shared History Cabinet.

The Original remains the most detailed product. It owns the complete research record, including raw sources, claims, component observations, causal links, story versions, charts, editorial decisions and post-event reviews.

The current semantic and presentation contract is defined in [Regime / Story Operating Framework](08_REGIME_STORY_OPERATING_FRAMEWORK.md). Regimes organise the durable market environment; Stories remain the canonical living hypotheses; What's New remains the dated delta layer; Hybrid remains presentation and learning only.

Production implementation must also satisfy [Regime Implementation Hardening & Failure Modes](09_REGIME_IMPLEMENTATION_HARDENING.md), including single-source System 1 sensors, pinned projection manifests, idempotency, time-skew visibility, lifecycle governance, shadow rollout and rollback.

## Product hierarchy

### Level 1: Editorial command surface

This is what Lee sees first.

It answers:

1. What changed since the last visit?
2. What matters most now?
3. What should be watched today, tonight or later in the week?
4. What does the market appear to be pricing?
5. What is confirmed, contradicted or still missing?
6. What deserves an OB, COTD, monitor or no action?

### Level 2: Research workbenches

Specialist pages provide the full detail:

- Latest Data Releases;
- Release Lab;
- ISM component room;
- Inflation and Fed reaction function;
- Evidence Room;
- Statement comparison;
- Physical confirmation;
- Earnings expectations room;
- Chart room;
- Catalyst calendar;
- Article memory.

### Level 3: Regime, sub-regime and persistent Story state

This layer connects evidence across time without flattening every mechanism into one Story list.

The initial governed Regimes are:

- Sovereign Funding & Global Cost of Capital;
- US-China AI Industrial Competition;
- Global Energy Security & Inflation;
- Gold & Global Reserve Diversification;
- Equity Rally Quality & Earnings Breadth.

Each Regime contains controlled sub-regimes/drivers. Stories are the current living interpretations inside those drivers and may link to more than one Regime. The visible Story headline may change with a material accepted thesis version while the canonical Story identity and history remain stable.

Regimes update from canonical Story reasoning, never directly from raw headlines.

### Level 4: History Cabinet and audit trail

Every release, story, state and article decision is retrievable by vintage.

## Recommended homepage

### A. Since Last Visit / What's New

Only material deltas. This is the dated change feed, not the persistent Story registry.

Required fields:

- time;
- event;
- exact new fact;
- verification status;
- Story affected;
- Regime and sub-regime affected;
- contribution: supports / contradicts / unresolved / context;
- why it matters;
- market reaction;
- editorial action change.

### B. What Matters Now

One centrepiece story.

Required blocks:

- event headline;
- current thesis;
- what changed;
- expectation map;
- why the market may be right;
- overlooked variable;
- evidence for;
- evidence against;
- mechanism;
- market pricing versus reality;
- next catalyst;
- confirmation;
- invalidation;
- relevant assets;
- best chart;
- editorial recommendation.

### C. Today, Tonight and Later This Week

Calendar events are grouped by when they can alter an active story.

Each event includes:

- local time;
- consensus or expected decision;
- prior and revision risk;
- active stories affected;
- surprise thresholds;
- most relevant assets;
- what outcome would change the view.

### D. Editorial Queue

Three to seven items maximum:

- Lead now;
- Develop today;
- Prepare conditional;
- COTD;
- Add to OB;
- Wait;
- Monitor.

Cooling and resolved stories sit below the active queue.

### E. Latest Data Release

A compact release card links to the full workbench.

It shows:

- actual, consensus, prior and revision;
- decisive component;
- Inflation check;
- Fed check;
- relevant asset;
- state change;
- story update.

### F. Regime State Delta

A compact strip shows only Regimes or sub-regimes that materially changed. Complex Regimes such as Rates use coloured subgroup tabs/cards rather than long row lists.

The rates Regime must distinguish at minimum:

- Fed / Front End;
- Treasury / Fiscal;
- Long End / Term Premium;
- Global Rates / Japan;
- Credit / Financing;
- Housing / Real Economy.

Each subgroup shows its current state, direction, confidence, current Stories, deciding monitors, latest contributing evidence/news nodes and next test.

The same subgroup pattern applies to AI, Energy, Gold and Equities using their governed dictionaries.

### G. Supporting modules

Market Pulse, Story Map, Top Signals, Trend Preview, Article Memory and system health remain, but sit beneath the decision surface.

## ISM component room

The ISM room includes:

1. Headline PMI.
2. New Orders.
3. Production.
4. Employment.
5. Supplier Deliveries.
6. Inventories.
7. Prices.
8. Backlog of Orders.
9. New Export Orders.
10. Imports.

Optional services-specific components can be configured separately.

### Required interpretation sequence

1. Identify the main driver of the headline move.
2. Determine whether demand and output agree.
3. Determine whether Employment confirms or lags.
4. Explain whether slower Supplier Deliveries reflect demand or disruption.
5. Explain whether Prices indicate broadening pipeline pressure.
6. Determine whether inventory behaviour is planned or unwanted.
7. Compare with regional surveys, payrolls, claims, CPI, PPI and market pricing where relevant.
8. State whether the release changes the Fed path.
9. Name only the directly relevant assets.
10. Store the vintage in the History Cabinet.

## Relevance gate for asset impacts

An asset is displayed only when all of the following are true:

1. The causal link is direct enough to explain in one sentence.
2. The horizon is specified.
3. The asset is currently sensitive to the relevant driver.
4. The move or pricing can be checked.
5. The asset adds information rather than filling a template.

The system should prefer three strong asset links over ten generic arrows.

## Processing and maintenance

### One write path

All writes occur in the Original.

### Derived views

Original pages and Hybrid pages use generated presentation snapshots.

### Release templates

Every release family has a stable component dictionary and cross-check map.

### Story templates

Every Story uses the same Decision Brief schema, with optional specialist modules. The Story ID/slug and historical lineage are stable, while the current visible headline may change after a material accepted thesis revision.

### Regime projection

A post-Story projection layer maps the accepted current Story version into one or more governed Regimes/sub-regimes. It deduplicates canonical evidence by evidence ID and source ancestry, aggregates Story monitors upward, and versions the Regime only when its state materially changes.

### Hybrid deep links

Where the exact target is available, Live should link directly to:

- `/hybrid-output?regime=<regime-slug>`;
- `/hybrid-output?story=<story-slug>`;
- `/hybrid-output?event=<event-id>`.

Hybrid may simplify and teach the causal chain but must preserve the exact canonical claim status, confidence, evidence state, confirmation and invalidation.

### State mappings

A small governed dictionary maps evidence to macro states.

### Four scheduled cycles

- 00:40 video and overnight intake;
- 08:30 full desk;
- 11:30 refresh;
- 22:00 evening delta.

### Event-driven updates

Triggered by:

- high-impact release;
- policy decision;
- earnings event;
- verified material statement;
- physical-flow change;
- large cross-asset move;
- contradiction to a priority story.

### Failure handling

The interface displays:

- stale source;
- missing release component;
- unresolved contradiction;
- failed market-data fetch;
- delayed transcript;
- cabinet write failure;
- Hybrid snapshot lag.

It must never silently reuse an old conclusion as current.

## Acceptance tests

### ISM test

The page must explain whether a headline move came from demand, production, supply delays, prices or inventories, and whether that changes inflation, Fed pricing or only the manufacturing view.

### NFP test

The page must integrate headline payrolls, revisions, unemployment, wages and hours with claims, JOLTS, Treasury pricing and active Fed or yen stories.

### Oman or Iran deal test

The desk must identify the reported framework, parties, stage, contradictions, implementation requirements, physical confirmation, market pricing, timeline and next checkpoint.

### Maintenance test

A revised value updates the Original, the affected Story, the relevant Regime/sub-regime projection, Hybrid and History Cabinet without manual duplication. A raw headline cannot bypass canonical Story reasoning to mutate a Regime.

### Story / What's New test

A fresh event may appear in What's New without creating a new Story. A current Story headline changes only after a material accepted Story-version revision, while its ID, slug and history remain stable.

### Rates visual test

The rates Regime renders as subgroup tabs/cards rather than one long list, and each subgroup shows current Stories, contribution nodes and the next deciding test.

### Cross-Regime test

One canonical Story may appear under more than one Regime without duplicating evidence, events or thesis history.

### Single-source System 1 test

Rates displayed in Dossier and the Regime surface derive from the same deterministic contract. No second Rates calculator exists.

### Projection integrity test

Repeated or concurrent projection of the same pinned Story/sensor manifest is idempotent and cannot create conflicting Regime versions.

### Degraded-state test

Fresh System 1 telemetry, older System 2 interpretation and projector/Hybrid lag remain separately visible rather than being collapsed into a false-current state.

### Rollback test

Regime UI, automatic routing, automatic Regime versioning and Hybrid deep links can be disabled independently without breaking Stories, What's New, Dossier or Journey.

### Usefulness test

Within sixty seconds, Lee can identify the lead story, the decisive evidence, the next catalyst and the recommended output.

## Final recommendation

The Original should feel like a complete institutional research room with an editor at the front desk.

Depth remains available everywhere. The difference is that the system now processes that depth into a timely editorial decision before asking the user to navigate the supporting equipment.
