# V8 Navigation and Information Architecture

## Final navigation pattern

V8 uses two visible rows.

### Desk

- Overview
- What's New
- Stories
- Articles
- Hybrid Output

### Data and tools

- Macro Data
- Heatmaps
- Positioning
- Charts
- History

On narrow screens, the rows may scroll horizontally. Do not hide the destinations behind nested dropdowns.

## Route ownership

### Overview

Owns:

- research system health;
- last and next research run;
- source failures and stale checks;
- active Story map;
- latest material changes;
- Hybrid readiness;
- shortcuts into retained specialist workspaces.

It does not repeat every page in miniature.

### What's New

Owns:

- raw and verified intake;
- statements;
- news threads;
- new videos and transcript-derived candidates;
- release deltas;
- market moves;
- duplicates;
- promotion and rejection states.

### Stories

Owns:

- persistent narrative registry;
- Story detail;
- event log;
- evidence balance;
- related releases, charts, statements, COT and articles;
- confirmation, invalidation and next catalyst.

### Articles

Owns:

- published coverage memory;
- Story matching;
- what changed since publication;
- duplication detection;
- revisit recommendation.

### Macro Data

Owns:

- current release stack;
- day synthesis;
- release interpretation;
- Component Laboratory;
- calendar linkage;
- twelve-month search;
- revisions and historical vintages.

### Heatmaps

Owns:

- macro state;
- market state;
- breadth state;
- historical percentile scans;
- category filters;
- detail drawers.

### Positioning

Owns:

- official CFTC participant view;
- COTSignal-style Legacy View;
- weekly extremes;
- weekly insights;
- market drill-down;
- Story relevance.

### Charts

Owns:

- active Story charts;
- rates;
- macro;
- indices;
- breadth;
- FX;
- commodities;
- credit;
- selected positioning charts.

### History

Owns:

- append-only versions;
- research ledger;
- prior Story states;
- macro vintages;
- method changes;
- post-event lessons;
- Hybrid snapshot history.

### Hybrid Output

Owns:

- approved outgoing snapshot;
- exclusions;
- blockers;
- destination mapping;
- quality gates;
- exact Original links.

## Specialist functions

Specialist workspaces may exist as secondary routes or drawers when their depth justifies it:

- Oil System;
- Earnings and Guidance;
- Economic Calendar full view;
- Research Registry and source monitor;
- Statements monitor.

They should be reachable contextually from Overview, What's New, Stories or Data. They should not crowd the primary navigation.
