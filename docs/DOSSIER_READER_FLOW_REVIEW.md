# Dossier reader-flow review

This applies to the existing Live storyline composer's opening and storyline summaries. It does not change Story research, selection, immutable lesson bodies, evidence rules, the output schema or Hybrid's read-only role.

Source direction: the Alchemy Markets chats **Clean Up Article Logic**, **Research Article Generation**, **Review Tesla Article** and **Tesla Article Research**, reviewed 5 September 2026. The user asks for direct narrative, supporting statistics, clear mechanisms and conditional outcomes, with no suspense, artificial contrasts or internal notes. The Black Desk visual samples were not available in the retrieved chats; this change makes no visual-design assumptions.

## Editorial acceptance

Review an actual generated opening and storyline summary against the exact canonical input:

1. The opening states the event or current condition and its relevance directly. A continuing Story is not represented as a new event.
2. Each sentence answers a question raised by the preceding explanation. The output does not mechanically recite a fixed set of headings.
3. Every number and comparison exists in the input, with its timeframe and qualification preserved.
4. Each causal claim retains its supplied evidential strength. Alternative outcomes remain conditional.
5. Claims about observed price reaction require supplied tape. Missing tape is not permission to assume the expected reaction occurred.
6. The strongest relevant countercase or break condition remains visible.
7. Remove hype, suspense, repeated punchlines, generic transitions and references to internal processing.

## Synthetic review cases

These examples illustrate wording only. They are not current market facts, model inputs or templates to copy into production.

### Released data with mixed signals

Supplied: payrolls exceed consensus; wage growth eases; front-end yields rise; equities finish little changed; inflation is the next stated policy catalyst.

Acceptable direction: "Payrolls exceeded expectations while wage growth eased. The stronger employment reading supports the supplied case for restrictive policy, although softer wages leave the inflation outlook unresolved. Front-end yields rose and equities finished little changed. The next inflation release will help distinguish between the two policy scenarios."

Reject: "The market was given every reason to collapse. And yet it refused. The real test starts now." This adds certainty, a dramatic expected outcome and filler.

### Local comparison with limited precision

Supplied: one ride-fare comparison is cheaper; fleet size remains small; sustained operating costs and regulatory outcomes are unknown.

Acceptable direction: "The quoted ride was cheaper in this comparison. That could help attract passengers, but one quote does not establish normal fares or operating profitability. Fleet expansion and sustained costs remain the tests of the business case."

Reject: "The cheaper service proves the incumbent's business model is broken." A single observation does not support that conclusion.

### Missing market tape

Supplied: an active inflation Story and its policy transmission hypothesis; no current price observations.

Acceptable direction: explain the continuing inflation-to-policy mechanism conditionally. Do not say that bonds sold off or equities absorbed the news.

## Validation boundary

The repository test suite and production build establish integration compatibility. They do not prove prose quality. The next normal composed edition must pass the editorial review above before this change is described as a demonstrated output-quality improvement. Historical editions and existing lesson bodies are not rewritten by this change.
