## Read the contradiction, not just the print
1. **Fed versus Treasuries:** Sticky inflation reduces the plausibility of near-term *Fed cuts*, but safe-haven demand can *raise Treasury prices* at the same time. Official 29 Oct Treasury observations were collected before Amazon's post-close report. Do not attribute that session's 7bp fall to earnings published later.
2. **AI versus broad equities:** AWS customer demand is accelerating, while Amazon's **trailing** FCF is a $42.4bn outflow. A negative shareholder cash-flow score for capital-heavy buyers does not negate profitable MSFT or chip suppliers. Nasdaq weakness on 29 Oct coexisted with **most individual US stocks rising**. That is a breadth/leadership divergence, not proof that every equity should fall.
3. **Energy versus gold:** Middle-distillate scarcity can maintain an inflation shock, but gold can decline while the dollar and yields also retreat. Do not force every geopolitically sensitive market into the same direction.
4. **JPY versus universal carry liquidation:** BoJ's hawkish hold, 8–1 vote and lower US yields favour JPY; they do not prove hedge-fund deleveraging, basis-swap stress or forced international selling.
5. **China versus global risk:** Chinese electronics-producer profitability is much stronger than the wider investment backdrop. It does *not* prove that Chinese models generate superior gross profit per delivered workload.
6. **Housing versus credit crisis:** High mortgage costs and reduced starts are independently bearish for affordability and construction. Existing-home sales improved sequentially. A confirmed bank credit event requires separate charge-offs/delinquencies, collateral and funding evidence.

## Limitations and refresh policy
- **No new scheduler**, unqualified research engine, creator-to-public promotion, private Notion leakage, or System-2 canonical status change.
- Each lens's meter is a dated manual judgement. The separate public source reader and Market Monitor may update but must not silently flip it. A new policy release, issuer result or contradictory price action should trigger **existing editorial reassessment** before the public score changes.
- Do not aggregate conflicting time windows. 29 Oct Treasury closing marks precede AMZN's 29 Oct after-hours results; 30 Oct Asian trading is a separate session. A one-day market return is not a 3-month regime forecast.
- **Meaning of confidence:** high = current directional conditions well documented by originals; medium = material evidence with live countercase; low = serious missing drivers. It is not a backtested win-rate or numeric probability.
- Detailed Ueda/Q&A and event-matched FX; 29–30 Oct 5m/30m/4h gold/yield/breadth tapes; final Treasury-real-yield confirmations; actual Nasdaq equal-weight/A-D line; China model gross-margin data; credit borrower-level refinance exposures still require independent checks. Neither absence nor silence is a zero observation.
