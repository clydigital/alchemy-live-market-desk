**Authority:** A post-decision, human-reviewed editorial addendum, not automatic publication to System-2/Dossier, a full live-data monitor or an instruction to change the existing five scheduled tasks. Evidence dates below are separated across US, Asia and each provider.

## 1. Japan / JPY: a hawkish hold actually moved FX

The BoJ held 1.25% by an 8–1 vote on 30 October. Hajime Takata wanted 1.50%, and the inflation outlook and future-hike language turned the hold into an arguably hawkish decision. Sources: [BoJ statement catalogue](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/mpmdeci/state_2026/) and [Reuters actual decision](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/boj-keeps-interest-rates-steady-signals-further-rate-hikes-2026-10-30/).

[Reuters Asia FX chronology](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/yen-steady-before-boj-ai-jitters-drive-treasury-rally-weigh-dollar-2026-10-30/) printed USDJPY 157.115. A separate Financial Modeling Prep forex-quote observation registered 157.305 at 30 October 04:21 UTC. These are different observation clocks; JPY did not stay at the initial trough. US Treasury safe-haven buying was a rival dollar driver.

Independent Financial Modeling Prep daily data through the last completed high-volume 28 Oct FX bar gave USDJPY close 157.810. A calculation across 193 weekday closes (from Feb 2026) gave EMA20 157.730, EMA50 157.930 and EMA100 158.314. Later 30 Oct quotes were below those earlier *daily references*. The 29–30 Oct vendor daily rows were sparse/partial and excluded from indicator calibration. Do not call the later quote a finished 4H bar or say the whole carry complex liquidated.

## 2. Gold: the yields-versus-bullion contradiction now has a number

The saved [US Treasury-linked daily real curve](https://tipsyield.com/30-year-tips-yield) records 10Y real yield 2.67% on 28 Oct and 2.63% on 29 Oct (down 4bp). The official 10Y nominal Treasury curve closed 5.05% then 4.98%; simple same-date nominal-minus-real was therefore 2.38% then 2.35%. [DXY historical prices](https://www.investing.com/indices/usdollar-historical-data?cid=1224074) fell 102.19 to 102.04. Gold nevertheless declined. [Reuters 30 Oct](https://www.reuters.com/world/india/gold-slips-higher-oil-us-inflation-data-bolster-fed-hike-bets-2026-10-30/) observed spot ~$3,997.39.

Two different commodity-price services independently signal a bearish daily technical stance. [MakeEA's dated gold FUTURES chart](https://makeea.com/markets/xauusd), 29 Oct 23:07 UTC, reports a 4H downtrend with EMA20 ~$4,043.85 and EMA50 ~$4,066.91. Its 30 Oct 00:07 UTC daily snapshot shows EMA20 ~$4,103.78 and EMA50 ~$4,187.31. A separate FMP GCUSD FUTURES series through the completed 29 Oct day reported $4,031.4 against calculated daily EMA20 ~$4,131 and EMA50 ~$4,224 based on ~193 weekday observations. These are NOT guaranteed identical spot XAUUSD prices or comparable exact stop levels. The missing order-flow provenance cannot be recreated from a chart.

## 3. US equity breadth: one big stock is not the whole index

[AP's 29 Oct close](https://apnews.com/article/2c26b0b1bbc3bfab9f3f4b902172f458): Nasdaq ~−1.4%, Russell 2000 ~+1%, and most equities advanced. [Dean's S&P constituent breadth](https://deanfi.com/insights/marketpulse/2026-10-29/) counted 406 advancers versus 97 decliners; the monitored 50DMA fraction was 63.6%, versus 57.3% the prior session; 20DMA 74.6% and 200DMA 60.6%. [The Daily Tape](https://stocks.pkladog.com/reports/2026-10-29/) reported RSP +1.20%, SPY −0.38% and 9 of 11 advancing sectors. A [broader 4,555-issue universe](https://www.thetrading.tools/advance-decline-line) counted 2,735 advancers and 1,707 decliners, ~60% positive.

These are different stock universes and calculated methods. The broad-universe 63-session positive fraction was just 47.6%; one strong day cannot establish a sustained all-clear.

## 4. Credit: a dated spread, not a conjectured crisis

[ICE BofA HY OAS data through FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/BAMLH0A0HYM2), [28 Oct dated observation](https://equibles.com/economicdata/bamlh0a0hym2/2026-10-28): 3.14 percentage points / 314bp on 28 Oct, versus 318bp on 27 Oct and 340bp on 22 Oct. The reading was thus tightening into the observed 28 Oct date. This is one session older than 29 Oct US breadth and does NOT prove what credit spreads did after the 29 Oct after-hours earnings. Also, ICE/BofA source licensing restricts redistribution of full priced series; summarise and link rather than republising their dataset.

## 5. Fed versus DXY: stronger inflation, weaker dollar

The BEA's September headline PCE was +3.6% YoY and the Fed held 3.75–4.00% with two hike dissents on 28 Oct. DXY settled around 102.19 on 28 Oct then 102.04 on 29 Oct. Its 30 Oct quoted intraday value was roughly 101.72, not a completed daily settlement. [Historical reference](https://www.investing.com/indices/usdollar-historical-data?cid=1224074). That makes an interesting divergence: inflation can constrain Fed cuts while the dollar weakens when US Treasury yields fall and foreign central banks turn more hawkish.
