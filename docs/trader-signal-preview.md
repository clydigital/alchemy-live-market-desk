# Trader-signal What’s New preview

Preview-only pass for analyst/trader review. Production remains unchanged until the preview is approved.

## What changes

- What’s New surfaces trader-facing risk flags and inline keyword emphasis for Fed/rates, inflation, labour, war risk, FX intervention, capex, valuation/bubble, liquidity/squeeze and urgent market language.
- Thesis revisions read as human research findings with the Story title, current thesis and previous thesis instead of storage/event-log wording.
- Generic event labels are humanised, for example Thesis revised, Story updated, Confirmed and Contradiction.
- FX intervention matching is scoped to currency/Japan/Treasury context so unrelated policy interventions do not get a false FX flag.

## Research boundary

Flags are a visual scan layer only. They do not mutate canonical Story fields, confidence, evidence state or published asset impacts.
