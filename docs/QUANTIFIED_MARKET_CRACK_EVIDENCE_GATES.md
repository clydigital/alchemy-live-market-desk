## Required contract for *each* measurable hypothesis

- **Target:** exact company, security, parent, subsidiary or supply/customer relationship; Regime slug, subgroup and existing Story/Investigation ID. Never infer a commercial relationship from similar stock performance.
- **Observable:** numerator, denominator, units (bps, %, $), accounting definition, applicable quarter/rolling window, seasonality, accounting changes, and instrument; report actual, previous, delta, threshold distance, as-of and source date. P/E is **not applicable** when forward earnings are non-positive.
- **Rule:** independently defined **watch**, **escalation**, **falsifier**, *minimum persistence window* and comparator. Thresholds below are PROVISIONAL research tripwires, not statistically calibrated crash forecasts; calibrate with a 3–5-year comparable history and sector distributions before automating a probability/status.
- **Evidence:** issuer SEC/IR filings or earnings transcripts for company financials; matched market observations for prices/spreads; official U.S. Treasury/FRED/Fed/ICE-licensed data when relevant. Source URL, publisher, original event time, freshness, canonical Evidence UUIDs, and quality/independence. Creator opinions are leads only.
- **Interpretation:** a breach is an investigation candidate, not proof of causation or an automatic Regime/Story downgrade. Require two independently sourced, economically different confirming channels for a major thesis change. Deterioration in price alone never establishes order cancellations, debt stress, or AI unit economics.
- **Missing value:** explicitly UNRESOLVED / DATA GAP; never assume zero, flat, continuing strength or that a threshold was passed.

## Failure modes illustrated by the three newly supplied transcripts

1. **Debt / dollar-reset transcript:** Rising Treasury yields with a weaker dollar are a valid divergence investigation. The narrator's claims of a coordinated intentional devaluation, foreign Treasury buyer strike, and buybacks as an ability to 'set' yields are hypotheses, not established evidence. A **weaker DXY is not mathematically equivalent to domestic inflation**; domestic price-level inflation can reduce the real burden of fixed-rate nominal debt, while higher refinancing rates offset savings. Treasury off-the-run buybacks are distinct from Federal Reserve QE/yield-curve control. Verify Treasury buyback *operations and accepted amounts* against official releases, auctions and TIC before any policy narrative. See US Treasury 19 Aug 2026: https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0607 and Reuters 1 Oct 2026: https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/treasurys-smaller-than-expected-buybacks-fuel-debate-over-aims-2026-10-01/
2. **Market-crash-conversation transcript:** The earnings guidance miss, 15% possible equity decline, French/Italian institutional accident, AI IPO failure, and XLF leadership comparisons are *scenario suggestions*. The 15% decline is not a forecast with measured likelihood. Track actual revisions, XLF-SPY relative performance, French OAT-Bund spreads, HY/CCC OAS and issuance; identify the exact bank/issuer before asserting a hidden failure.
3. **TraderNick transcript:** Creator says gold short was closed after the indicator became neutral, and he is long USDJPY. This **supersedes his earlier self-reported short stance** as a creator view, not as independently established market structure or a recommendation. USDJPY ~157.6/158.4 and gold ~4100 are *dated creator chart levels*; they must not be treated as live price or official invalidation. His TLT-put interpretation is a conditional hedge thesis; falling yields may result from growth stress, not necessarily lower AI borrowing demand. Strip promotional product claims from research.
