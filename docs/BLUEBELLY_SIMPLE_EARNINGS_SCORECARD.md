# Bluebelly — Simple Earnings Scorecard (v1, 28 October 2026)

**Status:** Approved recurring editorial method for **future company earnings discussion** in MacroPulse, Research Gap, AI Bubble regime checkpoints, Writing Brain commissioning and owner-facing Bluebelly explainers. This is a presentation/research contract, **not** an automatic source adapter, a claim that every historical report is updated, or a new scheduler. Use the shortest scorecard that answers the user's actual question.

## What a reader should grasp in 15–30 seconds

Open with **one plain-English verdict**: *"Sales beat expectations, profit was up, but cash generation weakened because spending grew faster."* Only say this if the cited figures support all parts.

For a material results release, show a **compact five- or six-line scorecard**:

| Question | Readable answer |
| --- | --- |
| **Beat or miss?** | Revenue and earnings (EPS or operating profit) **beat / met / missed / no comparable consensus**. Give actual vs **pre-release** consensus if independently sourced, with currency and percentage or absolute gap. |
| **Revenue and profit** | Actual revenue, operating profit (and EPS if relevant), and **up / down / flat** versus **year-ago equivalent quarter**; add sequential/QoQ only if it materially changes the story. State % and period. |
| **Free cash flow (FCF)** | Amount, sign, **up / down** versus same comparable quarter; cash flow from operations minus cash capital expenditures where consistently measurable. If the issuer uses a different definition, identify it. |
| **Capital expenditure (capex)** | Actual cash capex **up / down** versus year ago and whether **full-year planned spending/guidance was raised, cut or unchanged**; never confuse planned spending with cash already spent. |
| **Outlook and demand** | Did management raise, lower or maintain guidance? Note one relevant driver such as HBM demand, pricing, shipments, margins, AI data-centre demand or customer concentration. |
| **So what?** | One sentence connecting **sales → profit → cash conversion → capex burden → investor implications**; strongest counterpoint and the next number that would confirm/change the view. |

**First-use template, not invented SK hynix figures:**

> **SK hynix Q3 results:** Revenue [amount] — [beat/miss/not comparable] versus [pre-release consensus or unavailable]; [up/down] [X%] YoY. Operating profit [amount] — [up/down] [Y%]. FCF [amount or not disclosed] — [up/down or unknown]. Capex [amount or not disclosed] — [up/down] versus [period], with spending guidance [raised/cut/unchanged/not disclosed]. **Meaning:** [one clear cash-conversion and HBM business implication], **but** [countercase]; next watch [dated guidance/capex/FCF observation].

Do **not** repeat the identical fixed table multiple times in a long article. Put the compact scorecard at the **first earnings mention**, then integrate the causal explanation into normal LY narrative paragraphs.

## Strict measurement and source rules

1. **Issuer first.** Source earnings slides, regulatory filing, investor-relations release and original earnings-call transcript; record fiscal period, reported currency/unit (e.g. KRW trillion vs billion), publication and observation date. Prefer verified original release to third-party copy.
2. **"Beat" requires a comparator.** An actual result is not a "beat" without an available **pre-release** consensus for the same metric, fiscal period, accounting basis and currency. Quote the consensus provider and its as-of date. EPS is not interchangeable with operating profit, and a beat does not establish stock-price reaction.
3. **Direction must identify a baseline.** Mark YoY, QoQ or prior guidance for every **up/down/flat** call, and calculate the percentage only where both bases are genuinely comparable; if accounting, acquisitions, currencies or period lengths differ, label that limitation.
4. **FCF discipline.** Distinguish operating cash flow (CFO), capex cash paid, issuer-defined FCF, and net change in cash. Never infer cash flow from reported profit or confuse debt/equity proceeds with FCF. State the exact issuer definition for adjusted/alternative FCF and do not silently combine non-comparable definitions.
4a. **Negative cash flow is not automatically getting worse.** For FCF moving from -5 to -3 in the same units, describe it as **less negative / improving**, not as positive FCF or a new profit. For -3 to -5, say **more negative / deteriorating**. If percentage change across zero is misleading, use the absolute delta and direction instead.
5. **Capex precision.** Separate **cash spending during the quarter**, **equipment orders/commitments** and **next-year projected capex**. Compare capex to prior actuals or prior guidance, identifying forecast vs realised figures. "Higher capex" can reflect strong capacity investment, not automatically poor business economics; compare utilisation, return on incremental capital, financing cost and expected demand.
6. **Missing data stays missing.** Use **NOT REPORTED**, **NOT VERIFIED**, **NO COMPARABLE CONSENSUS** or **NOT YET RELEASED** instead of estimating or forcing directional arrows. A cash-flow statement may arrive after preliminary earnings; update the same source-linked entry then.
7. **Time and market reaction.** A report released after the close cannot have a verified same-session event reaction. Check dated post-release stock/sector/credit prices and distinguish stock movement from operating performance and expectations.
8. **Do not force a bubble conclusion.** A chip vendor can enjoy revenue/profit/FCF expansion while its customers incur heavy capex and delayed returns. Evaluate supplier profit separately from hyperscaler cash conversion, financing and demand. Positive FCF and higher capex are not mutually exclusive.
9. **Preserve privacy and controls.** Notion and Bluebelly owner reports may carry source-linked research candidates; do not automatically mutate canonical Stories or Regimes, publish client-facing articles, create new recurring tasks, or treat a generated sentence as independently verified.

## Length by surface

- **MacroPulse:** 1–3 lines, only for a material NEW earnings release; beat/miss, revenue/profit movement, FCF/capex movement or explicit absence, plus one question. Otherwise a source link and changed fact are enough.
- **Research Gap:** Verify the relevant missing figures; preserve numerator/denominator, consensus provenance and YoY/QoQ basis; record material contradiction and the next test. Do not re-research every issuer every time.
- **AI Bubble — 16 Companies:** Add a small scorecard when the issuer reports, updating the **existing child page**; distinguish capex commitments, operating cash flow, FCF, debt/net cash and funding dependence. For non-earnings days use **NO NEW MATERIAL RESULTS**, not a fake beat/miss.
- **Writing Brain / OB / COTD / daily report:** Show the compact first-use table or short paragraph for an earnings-led angle; then tell the market story, strongest counterargument and price confirmation/invalidation in conversational British English. Do not turn a chart-led technical note into a long financial statement summary.
- **Creator summary/presenter:** Attribute creator claims separately from issuer-confirmed results; explain FCF and capex in simple terms where the learning moment calls for it.

**Regression examples to verify in future:** SK hynix HBM earnings, Microsoft/Alphabet/Amazon hyperscaler AI spend, Nvidia chip margins, Meta/Oracle/CoreWeave financing. Source dates and reporting periods are mandatory. No prefilled live financial results are authorised by this style document.
