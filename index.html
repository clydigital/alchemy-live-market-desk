Story 01 · Equities / AI

AI spending is now a cash-conversion test

OPENING BELL LEAD

Market question

Why did Microsoft gain while Meta fell when both committed heavily to AI infrastructure?

Best explanation

Investors are differentiating by visible monetisation, backlog and profit conversion rather than rejecting capex itself.

Support

Microsoft reported $90.0bn revenue, $40.6bn operating income and 43% Azure growth.

Contradiction

Meta advertising demand remains healthy, so the negative reaction could prove too harsh if AI improves future ad yields.

What may be underpriced

Higher long yields make delayed monetisation more expensive. Capex-heavy companies may separate by how quickly spending reaches cash flow.

Story 02 · Rates / Fed

The hold concealed a long-end tightening shock

COTD LEAD

Market question

Why did long yields surge after the Fed left its target range unchanged?

Competing hypotheses

Hawkish policy repricing; inflation and oil term premium; Treasury supply or fiscal credibility pressure.

Best explanation

The front end recognised no immediate hike, while the long end priced a less comfortable inflation and credibility path.

Contradiction

The two-year yield softened. That weakens the case that the move represents a simple, broad Fed-hike repricing.

Confirmation

US30Y-US02Y remains steeper, credit weakens and oil or inflation data reinforce the move.

Verdict

Publish now. Provisional title: The Fed Held. Why Did Long Yields Surge?

Story 03 · Oil / Geopolitics

Escalation returned, but supply flows still matter more

MACRO BRIDGE

Market question

Why did crude fail to hold the full spike despite renewed attacks?

Best explanation

Headline risk is high, but functioning tanker flows and available supply are keeping the market below panic pricing.

Contradiction

Repeated attacks can quickly change freight, insurance or transit behaviour. Stable flows today do not remove future disruption risk.

Underpriced risk

Refined-product tightness can feed inflation even when crude itself does not revisit its high.

Confirmation

Freight, insurance and diesel or gasoline crack spreads rise alongside crude.

Verdict

Develop further. Use beneath the Fed story unless product data confirms a stronger standalone angle.
